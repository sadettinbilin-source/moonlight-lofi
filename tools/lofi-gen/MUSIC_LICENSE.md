# Music license

The music shipped with Moonlight Lofi is original.

Every track is synthesized from code in `lofi_gen.py`. The generator builds notes with oscillators, envelopes, and noise. It does not load samples, loops, stems, or third-party presets. The composition and the recording are owned completely by the creator of Moonlight Lofi. No other license applies.

The masters in `MoonlightLofi/Resources/Audio` are AAC at 256 kbps, 44.1 kHz, stereo, 150 seconds, loudness-normalized to −14 LUFS. Each file is written so the end joins the start. The player repeats that join without a gap when Loop one is on.

Do not replace these files with music you do not own.
